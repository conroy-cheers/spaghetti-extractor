## Immediate soundness guard

The audit confirmed that `pe32-module-deployment-v1` previously accepted a
complete behavioral-C receipt while the linked candidate could still contain only
the interpreter/native implementation. `write_pe32_module_deployment` now emits
the stable blocker `deployment_behavioral_c_not_linked` unless the link receipt
binds all of the following:

- Implementation kind `generated_behavioral_c`.
- The exact behavioral-C completion and machine-IR hashes.
- A nonempty, unique object-hash inventory.
- A nonempty, unique function-symbol inventory exactly equal to the linked symbols
  classified as generated behavioral C.

This deliberately leaves current source-only deployment claims incomplete until
the module-wide native link receipt and real behavioral-C link land in milestone 6.
